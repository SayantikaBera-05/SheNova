# ✨ GlowGram

**GlowGram** is an AI-powered content and curation studio built by **Team SheNova**. It transforms your photos and videos into personalized captions, hashtags, mood-based music recommendations, and Spotify playlists.

## 🚀 Features

* 🎨 **AI Vibe Analysis** — Detects the mood and aesthetic of your media.
* ✍️ **AI Captions** — Generates Aesthetic, Minimal, Storyteller, Viral, and more.
* #️⃣ **Smart Hashtags** — Creates categorized hashtags tailored to your content.
* 🎵 **Music Recommendations** — Finds songs and Spotify playlists matching your vibe.
* 🪄 **Customization Studio** — Use custom prompts to generate personalized captions and music suggestions.
* ⚡ **Instant Results** — Upload, analyze, customize, and create in seconds.
